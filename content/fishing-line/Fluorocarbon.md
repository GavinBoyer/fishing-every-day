# Fluorocarbon
### What is Fluorocarbon?

Fluorocarbon line, also known as 'fluoro,' is made just like [[monofilament]] except from much denser material, Teflon and Freon. In the 70s, when fluoro started appearing on angler's lines, it was very stiff and hard to use, making it only usable as a leader. Now, fluoro is one of the main picks for stealth anglers.

#### Pros and Cons of Fluorocarbon

**Pros**
- Fluoro's main strength, and what it is known for, is that it is virtually invisible under the surface.
- It is extremely abrasion resistant, making it the best choice for low maintenance and longevity, unlike [[braid]].
- Has a slight stretch, but only under significant pressure. This allows it to have high strength without precision taking a bump in quality.
- Fluoro is incredibly sensitive and gives feedback and taps even if the line is slack.

**Cons**
- [[Knots]] can fail if tied incorrectly.
- Extremely high memory, making it easily tangle and "bird's nest."
- Much more expensive than other types of [fishing line](fishing-line/index.md).
